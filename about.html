The company and its founder

The company, and the reporter who started it.

Pad & Pixel, LLC is an editorial consulting and content firm for the video game industry. It works for game companies, media publishers and brands.

Its founder, Brian Crecente, spent more than 15 years as a newspaper reporter. He started Kotaku and co‑founded Polygon.

The founder

Writing about games since 2000

Brian Crecente

Founder, Pad & Pixel, LLC

Brian founded Kotaku and co‑founded Polygon, which he helped take from start-up to one of the largest video game sites in the world. He was video games editor at Rolling Stone and at Variety.

Before he covered games full time, he spent more than 15 years as an award-winning reporter at major daily newspapers around the country, covering wildfires, daily crime, a contested presidential election and serial killers. For about a decade he also wrote a weekly gaming column for McClatchy-Tribune News Service.
