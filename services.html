Services

Twelve services in four practices

Editorial work for the games business.

Pad & Pixel is an editorial consulting and content firm for the video game industry. The work falls into four practices, and you can hire us for one service or several.

Clients have included game companies such as Epic Games and Niantic, media companies such as The Washington Post and Minute Media, and brands such as the LEGO Group and OtterBox.

The practices

What each service covers

01

Editorial & content

Writing and editing for anything that goes out under your name.

Copy editing
A careful read before anything goes public: articles, store copy, reports, press materials. We fix what is wrong and cut what is not needed.
Ghostwriting and content creation
Articles and longer pieces written for your byline or your channels. Pad & Pixel runs the news page for the Epic Games Store, where Brian Crecente is executive editor and helps lead a team of more than 50 freelancers.
Research
Answers to a specific question about games, players or the business, found and checked the way a reporter would.
White papers
A long paper that sets out findings or an argument for an industry audience. We can write it from your research or do the research too.
Editorial training
Training for the writers and editors you already employ. The aim is better work from your own team once we have gone.
02

Media & PR readiness

Brian Crecente was a newspaper reporter for more than 15 years before he covered games full time. This practice puts that experience on your side of the table.

Public relations
Help with announcements, press materials and dealing with reporters, from someone who spent years as the reporter on the other end.
Media training
Preparation for developers and executives before an interview or a public appearance. They practice with the questions they are likely to get, including the hard ones.
Disaster response training and post‑mortems
Planning for a bad day before it arrives, such as a broken launch or a leak, so your team knows who speaks and what they say. If it has already happened, an outside account of what went wrong and what to change next time.
03

Game analysis

A critic’s read of your game while there is still time to act on it.

Mock reviews

A private review of your unreleased game, written the way a critic would write it at launch and delivered under NDA. There are five formats, from a one-week read of a single demo or trailer to checkpoints from vertical slice through gold.

How mock reviews work
Pre- and post-launch game analysis
Before release, an assessment of what works, what does not and how the game is likely to land with critics and players. After release, a look back at how it was received and why.
04

On air

Brian Crecente has written about games since 2000 and has appeared on national TV and radio.

Podcasts
Hosting a podcast about games and the industry, or appearing on yours as a guest.
On-camera interviews
Interviews filmed for events or your own channels, with someone who has spent a career asking questions.
